# GSO-1 launch clip: brief

**Format:** 20 seconds, 4:5 (1080 x 1350), built with Remotion, rendered to `out/launch.mp4` and `cover.png`.

**The single action:** a click on "Personal Communication" in the GSO-1 sidebar, and every
conversation (Gmail, iMessage, Telegram, WhatsApp) lands in one inbox, already sorted by a
model running on the Mac.

**Headline (held 3 seconds at the end):**
"Every project. Every conversation. One staff officer."
Small line under it: "GSO-1 Pro · runs on your Mac · nothing leaves it"

**Beats:**
1. 0.0 to 3.0 s: the GSO-1 Ops Room enters (projects running, ports, the day's events).
2. 3.0 to 5.0 s: the cursor glides to the sidebar and taps "Personal Communication".
3. 5.0 to 10.0 s: four app dots fly in and land as rows in one inbox; "Needs reply" chips appear.
4. 10.0 to 14.0 s: a conversation opens; a draft writes itself in the reply box ("Draft · Friendly").
5. 14.0 to 17.0 s: the Local LLM card glows: "Qwen3-VL 8B · on this Mac".
6. 17.0 to 20.0 s: headline holds over a soft blur of the inbox.

**Data shown:** only made-up people and messages (Sam Lee, Priya Nair, "Dinner Saturday at 7?").
Never the owner's real inbox: the video is published on the public website.

**Style:** GSO-1's dark theme and colour tokens, Inter for text, the existing indigo accent.
No voice-over; a quiet tick on the tap.
